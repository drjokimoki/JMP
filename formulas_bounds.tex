{\Large\textbf{When does RSM beat both benchmarks?}}
\vspace{8pt}

\textbf{The three losses.}
\begin{align*}
  L^{\mathrm{ave}} &= s + \Qave &&\text{(no inflation)}\\[2pt]
  L_k^{\mathrm{bag},\infty} &= (A+\Rk)\,\frac{T-1}{T-k}, \quad A = s+\Qopt\\[2pt]
  L^{\mathrm{full}} &= A\,\frac{T-1}{T-m} &&\text{(full estimated optimal)}
\end{align*}

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{1. RSM beats the full estimated optimal.}

Exact equivalence:
\[
  L_k^{\mathrm{bag},\infty} < L^{\mathrm{full}}
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \Rk \;<\; A\cdot\frac{m-k}{T-m}.
\]

Certified sufficient condition, using the exact Jensen ceiling
$\Rk\le\Qsub-\Qopt$ from Proposition~7 (the exact bracket):
\[
  \boxed{\;\Qsub-\Qopt \;<\; A\cdot\dfrac{m-k}{T-m}\;}
\]

Leading order ($R_k^{\mathrm{ceil}}\approx B\zk$, $\zk=\tfrac{m-k}{km}$, valid for
$1\ll k\ll m$) — the $(m-k)$ factor cancels on both sides:
\[
  B\cdot\frac{m-k}{km} \;<\; A\cdot\frac{m-k}{T-m}
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \boxed{\;k\cdot m \;>\; \dfrac{B\,(T-m)}{A}\;}
\]

\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{2. RSM beats equal weighting.}

With $\Delta^{\mathrm{ave}} := \Qave - \Qopt = \dfrac{B(H-1)}{m}$ (exact):
\[
  L_k^{\mathrm{bag},\infty} < L^{\mathrm{ave}}
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  (A+\Rk)\,\frac{T-1}{T-k} \;<\; A + \Delta^{\mathrm{ave}}.
\]

Certified sufficient condition:
\[
  \boxed{\;\bigl(A + \Qsub - \Qopt\bigr)\,\dfrac{T-1}{T-k}
  \;<\; A + \dfrac{B(H-1)}{m}\;}
\]

Leading order, further taking $T\to\infty$ (so $\tfrac{T-1}{T}\to1$):
\[
  \boxed{\;k \;>\; \dfrac{m}{H-1}\;}
\]
\textcolor{gray}{\small (Sanity check: as $H\to1$ this threshold $\to\infty$ ---
no finite $k$ beats equal weighting, matching Remark~7 (the flat-risk case)'s
$k^*=1$ result exactly.)}

\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{Caveat.} The leading-order boxes use $\Qsub-\Qopt\approx B\zk$,
which the paper itself only claims is accurate for $1\ll k\ll m$
(the average-subset approximation's own stated scope). At small $k$ this drops
Lemma~4 (Taylor expansion)'s correction term
$\mathrm{Var}_S(A_S)/(k\bar\tau)^3$ and can understate the true ceiling.
The two \emph{exact} boxes (using $\Qsub$ directly) do not have this
problem and are valid at any $k$.
